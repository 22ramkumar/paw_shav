First Time Boarding?‎ 7 Steps to Prepare Your Dog for a Stress-Free Stay

Leaving your best friend for the first time can trigger intense travel guilt and separation anxiety—not just for your dog, but for you as the pet parent! The secret to a triumphant first stay lies in proactive familiarization. When a pet recognizes the sights, smells, and friendly faces of their caregivers, boarding transitions from an uncertain event into an exciting vacation retreat.

1. Book a Trial Daycare Play Session First

Never let overnight boarding be your dog's very first time through the facility doors. We always recommend booking a half-day daycare visit 1 to 2 weeks prior. This demonstrates to your dog that you always return to pick them up, turning the resort into a trusted playground.

2. Maintain Their Exact Home Diet & Portioning

Abrupt dietary changes combined with the excitement of a new environment can trigger sensitive stomach upsets. Pre-portion your dog's regular dry kibble or wet food into labeled zip-top bags with their daily feeding schedule clearly marked.

Pro Caretaker Tip:

Pack a couple of extra food portions just in case your return flight or travel itinerary gets delayed by weather.

3. Bring an Unwashed T-Shirt or Comfort Blanket

Canines experience the world primarily through their sense of smell. Sleeping with an unwashed t-shirt or towel carrying your personal scent releases calming endorphins, helping them fall soundly asleep on their luxury orthopedic mattress.

4. Update Vaccines At Least 10 Days in Advance

Vaccines require several days to build peak immune antibodies. Don't rush into a vet clinic the morning of your trip. Ensure Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella are administered at least 10 days before your check-in date.

5. Keep Your Drop-Off Goodbye Upbeat and Brief

Dogs are emotional barometers. If you hug them tightly, cry, or linger with an anxious voice, your dog assumes something dangerous is happening. Keep drop-off cheerful, quick, and confident! Give a quick chin scratch, hand over their leash with a smile, and say: "Have fun on vacation!"

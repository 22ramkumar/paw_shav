The PawsHaven Story

From One Family’s Search to a State-of-the-Art Resort

In 2016, our founders Jessica Miller and Dr. Marcus Vance traveled for an emergency family trip. Looking for a kennel for their two Golden Retrievers, they were heartbroken by what they found: noisy concrete enclosures, concrete floors, and zero individual attention.

They knew pet parents deserved better. They purchased 5 scenic acres in Austin, Texas and built PawsHaven: a facility designed with veterinary-grade HVAC air sanitization, private sound-dampened suites, natural grass agility fields, and real-time parent webcams.

Our Moral Compass

Principles We Never Compromise On

Every decision at PawsHaven—from suite ventilation to caregiver hiring—is anchored in six non-negotiable promises to your pets.

Pillar 01

Unconditional Empathy

We honor every dog and cat as an individual soul with unique emotions, quirks, and communication cues. Zero punishment, zero intimidation, 100% fear-free positive reinforcement.

Pillar 02

Clinical-Grade Biosecurity

Continuous medical-grade UV-C air scrubbers exchange room air every 7 minutes. Hospital sanitization protocols and strict vaccine oversight safeguard against canine cough and viral risks.

Pillar 03

Radical Transparency

No behind-closed-doors secrets. Parents receive 24/7 HD webcam streaming in private suites, live mobile activity updates, and an open walk-in policy for facility tours any day of the week.

Pillar 04

Low Supervision Ratios

We enforce an industry-leading 1:5 staff-to-pet ratio in our play meadows. Small, carefully screened groups matched by size and temperament ensure calm, joyful, and accident-free play.

Pillar 05

Holistic Enrichment

Physical exercise is only half the equation. Our sensory gardens, brain puzzles, bacon bubble sessions, and agility tunnels stimulate curious minds and reduce stress naturally.

Pillar 06

Shelter Philanthropy

We donate 5% of all boarding profits to Central Texas rescues and maintain four permanent foster suites on campus where homeless dogs receive rehabilitation while awaiting adoption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions from pet parents discovering PawsHaven for the very first time.

Can I visit and tour the 5-acre facility before booking?

Yes, absolutely! We maintain an open-door policy. You are welcome to drop by for a self-guided or staff-led tour any day between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM without an appointment. You can also schedule a private guided tour using our website anytime.

What makes PawsHaven fundamentally different from standard kennels?

Traditional kennels use metal chain-link cages, echoey concrete runs, and minimal staff time. PawsHaven features private tempered-glass luxury suites with orthopedic mattresses, hospital-grade UV-C air exchanges every 7 minutes, real grass play yards, 24/7 webcams, and an industry-best 1:5 staff-to-pet ratio.

How do you handle medical conditions, insulin, or emergencies?

All medications (oral, topical, and subcutaneous insulin) are administered strictly by Licensed Veterinary Technicians and time-stamped in your parent portal. Co-founder Dr. Marcus Vance, DVM is on call 24/7, and our campus is 4 minutes away from Central Austin Veterinary Emergency Hospital.

How do you group dogs for daycare and outdoor play?

Every dog completes a complimentary 30-minute temperament evaluation before entering group play. Groups are separated by size (teacup, medium, giant) and play energy style (gentle/seniors vs enthusiastic fetchers). If a pet prefers human one-on-one attention, we offer private luxury play packages.

Are cats completely separated from dog boarding areas?

100% yes. The Feline Penthouse Sanctuary is housed in an independent wing with acoustic sound-dampening walls, dedicated feline staff, and its own HVAC ventilation system so our cat guests never hear, see, or smell barking dogs.

What certifications and background checks do your staff complete?

Every staff member undergoes comprehensive background vetting and is 100% certified in Pet First Aid & CPR by the American Red Cross and Fear Free™ handling techniques. Our training team holds CPDT-KA certifications and our grooming salon is led by National Dog Groomers Association masters.

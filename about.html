The PawsHaven Story

From One Family’s Search to a State-of-the-Art Resort

In 2016, our founders Jessica Miller and Dr. Marcus Vance traveled for an emergency family trip. Looking for a kennel for their two Golden Retrievers, they were heartbroken by what they found: noisy concrete enclosures, concrete floors, and zero individual attention.

They knew pet parents deserved better. They purchased 5 scenic acres in Austin, Texas and built PawsHaven: a facility designed with veterinary-grade HVAC air sanitization, private sound-dampened suites, natural grass agility fields, and real-time parent webcams.

Health & Sanitation

Hospital-Grade Safety Protocols

Every inch of PawsHaven is engineered for physical safety, emotional calmness, and medical-grade cleanliness.

Hospital-Grade UV-C HVAC

Continuous air exchange system with UV-C scrubbers eliminates airborne bacteria, odors, and kennel cough viruses completely.

Filtered Spring Hydration

All drinking water is multi-stage filtered and served in sanitized stainless-steel bowls refreshed four times daily.

Double-Gated Security

Every play yard and suite zone has double airlock security gates, 8-foot perimeter fencing, and 24/7 security sensor alarms.

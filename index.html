Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your pet's stay, safety guidelines, and what to pack.

What vaccinations are required before boarding or daycare?‎

For dogs: Rabies, DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), and Bordetella (within the last 6 months). For cats: Rabies and FVRCP. All vaccines must be documented and submitted at least 48 hours prior to check-in.

Can I access the live webcams at any time of day?‎

Yes! Our yard webcams operate during play hours (7:30 AM to 7:00 PM), and private luxury suite webcams stream 24/7 with gentle night-vision so you can check on your sleeping pet anytime worldwide.

What should I pack for my pet's overnight stay?‎

We supply orthotic bedding, stainless steel bowls, and fresh linens. Please bring your pet's regular food pre-portioned in zip bags, any prescription medications in original bottles, and one favorite comfort blanket or chew toy smelling like home.

How do you handle medical emergencies or special diets?‎

Our licensed veterinary technicians administer oral, topical, and injectable medications according to strict schedules. We have Dr. Marcus Vance on-call 24/7 and our emergency hospital partner just 6 minutes away.

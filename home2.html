Got Questions?

Smart Resort Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our smart check-in, live webcams, dietary protocols, and safety guarantees.

How does the live webcam streaming work, and can multiple family members watch?
Once your reservation is confirmed, your pet portal account unlocks encrypted, high-definition 60FPS video feeds of your pet's private suite and the communal agility meadows. You can share access with family members securely via authorized email invites or the client dashboard.
What vaccinations and medical checks are required before check-in?
For canines, we require Rabies, DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), Bordetella, and the Bivalent Canine Influenza vaccine. For felines, Rabies and FVRCP are required. You can upload digital veterinary records directly to your account during booking.
How do you handle special dietary needs and prescription medications?
Our veterinary technicians administer oral, topical, and injectable medications (including insulin) strictly according to veterinary timestamps. Meals are weighed on digital scales and cross-checked against allergy alerts to guarantee zero dietary cross-contamination.
Can I tour the facility before booking my pet's stay?
Absolutely! We offer complimentary guided walkthroughs Monday through Saturday between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM. You can also view our interactive 360° yard explorer above or request a live video tour with our front-desk concierge.
What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
We understand plans change. Standard boarding reservations can be cancelled or rescheduled with a 100% refund up to 48 hours prior to check-in time. For major holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's), we request a 7-day notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Visitor Guidelines

Quick clarity on facility tours, check-in logistics, mandatory vaccinations, and 24/7 veterinary care.

1 Can I visit and tour the 5-acre resort before booking a stay?
Tours & Visits

Yes, absolutely! We welcome prospective pet parents with an open-door policy. You can drop in for a guided walkthrough of our dog suites, cat penthouse, splash park, and private agility yards any day Monday through Friday between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM without an appointment.

If you prefer a weekend tour or want to meet directly with our veterinary medical director or senior trainer, simply send a note through our contact form above or call our concierge desk at (555) 729-7428.

2 What are your check-in & check-out hours, and what if my flight is delayed?
Hours & Arrival

Our lobby is open Monday–Friday: 7:00 AM – 7:30 PM and Saturday–Sunday: 8:00 AM – 6:30 PM. Standard check-out time is 12:00 PM (noon) for overnight boarding guests.

Since we are situated just 10 minutes from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (ABIA), we know travel disruptions happen. If your flight is delayed, call or text our front desk before closing. We provide extended VIP pickup until 9:30 PM or will comfortably settle your pet into their suite for an extra night with fresh gourmet dinner and morning turnout.

3 What mandatory vaccinations and veterinary paperwork are required?
Vaccines & Health

To safeguard every guest in our sanctuary, we enforce strict veterinary verification:

  • Canine Guests: Rabies, DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), and Bordetella within the past 6 or 12 months. Bivalent Canine Influenza (H3N2/H3N8) is strongly encouraged.
  • Feline Guests: Rabies and FVRCP (Feline Viral Rhinotracheitis, Calicivirus, Panleukopenia).
  • Flea & Tick: Proof of current veterinary-grade parasite prevention.

You can upload digital certificates straight into your Parent Portal or have your vet office email records directly to records@pawshavenresort.com prior to arrival.

4 How does PawsHaven handle medical emergencies or special medication routines?
Medical & Care

Safety and medical excellence are built into our resort foundation. Our co-founder, Dr. Marcus Vance, DVM, supervises our health protocols and is on-call 24 hours a day. In addition, our campus is located under 5 minutes from Central Texas Veterinary Specialty & Emergency Hospital.

If your pet takes prescription pills, eye drops, or insulin injections, our Licensed Veterinary Technicians administer and log every dose with time-stamps in your parent portal. For urgent inquiries, our direct emergency vet hotline is (555) 911-PETS.
